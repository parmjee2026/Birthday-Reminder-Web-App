Google provides your contact data to this browser through the Google People API. Birthday Reminder does not maintain its own cloud database of your contacts.

Google Contacts remain read-only. You can clear saved device data or disconnect Google from Settings at any time.

Requested permission

The app asks only for read-only Google Contacts access.

The same read-only permission is used to show your display name through Google People API. No separate profile scope is requested. The app cannot edit or delete contacts.

Device data flow

Google Contacts → Google People API → your browser.

Device data flow

By default contacts are session-only. If "Remember contacts on this device" is enabled, a copy is saved in this device's IndexedDB so the installed app can reopen without Google login just to view the saved list.

No contact record is uploaded to the website owner.

Birthday-wish images remain session-only and are not stored in the device cache.

Requested permission

The app asks only for read-only Google Contacts access.

The same read-only permission is used to show your display name through Google People API. No separate profile scope is requested. The app cannot edit or delete contacts.

Clear immediately

Disconnect & Clear revokes the current Google access token and clears current page memory. Use "Clear Saved Device Data" in Settings to remove saved name, status and cached contacts from this device.

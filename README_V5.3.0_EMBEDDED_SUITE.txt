BIRTHDAY REMINDER v5.3.0 — EMBEDDED MY APP SUITE
=================================================

New user flow:
1. Birthday Reminder opens to My App Suite first.
2. Tap Birthday Reminder to enter login/dashboard.
3. Tap Naam Jaap Counter to switch to the other app.
4. Inside Birthday Reminder, tap ▦ to reopen My App Suite.

No third My App Suite app is required.
Google Contacts remains read-only (contacts.readonly).
No shared login/database/permission layer was added.
